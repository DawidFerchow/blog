**Sales Specialist / Sales Department Manager**

This was my first real job. I had almost no professional experience, but I was hired by a company that sold products on Allegro and eBay. My main task was to sell products on the German version of eBay.

I managed to develop the sales there, starting from a small online shop and helping it grow into a larger branch in a German town. Thanks to this, I was promoted to Sales Department Manager. I was responsible for sales on eBay, Allegro and Amazon.

**Sales responsibilities**

* Setting sales directions and goals
* Giving tasks to the right employees
* Watching competitors’ actions
* Reacting to competitors’ actions
* Training employees in sales
* Improving and automating work processes

**IT Specialist**

That was not everything. My boss knew about my IT education, so I was also responsible for buying and maintaining the company server. At first, the server was needed to host Sello.

Later, I was responsible for implementing Baselinker in the company. As the company grew, Sello was replaced by a semi-dedicated order management solution. This solution was Serini-cloud. I was responsible for the whole technical cooperation, including API access, server access and technical communication.

**IT responsibilities**

* Administration of company servers, including Windows Server
* Implementing new IT solutions, such as Baselinker
* Improving and automating work, for example automatic actions in Baselinker
* Administration of Sello, including updates, backups and database cleaning
* Solving problems with Sello
* Administration of Serini-cloud
