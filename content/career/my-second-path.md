**Sales Department Manager**

Sauneo sp. z o.o. was a natural next step in my professional development. In this company, I was responsible for the whole sales department. My work included building cooperation with distributors and managing the sales team.

**Sales responsibilities**

* Setting sales directions and goals
* Giving tasks to the right employees
* Watching competitors’ actions
* Reacting to competitors’ actions
* Training employees in sales
* Improving and automating work processes

**IT Specialist / Programmer**

In this company, my IT responsibilities became wider than in my previous job. I was also responsible for building, developing and administrating websites based on PrestaShop.

For the website sauneo.pl, a ready-made template was installed. I adapted and modified this template according to current needs, especially when some functions were not included in the original template.

For poolneo.pl, I created a child theme and used it to build our own custom template. On both websites, I also installed my own plugins. On sauneo.pl, one of my plugins showed assigned carriers on the product page.

For this company, I created the PriceChecker program from scratch. After adding competitors’ links, the program automatically checked their current prices. Later, I was also responsible for hosting. This was my biggest project, and I describe it in more detail here.

During my employment, the online shops worked on a Hetzner VPS server with Debian and LAMP. I created and administrated this server myself.

**IT responsibilities**

* Administration of a VPS server with Debian and LAMP
* Creating software to improve work, for example PriceChecker
* Modifying templates on websites based on PrestaShop
* Creating simple PrestaShop plugins, for example a plugin showing carriers assigned to a product
* Adding new elements to websites based on PrestaShop
* Integrating different platforms with different solutions, for example ERLI -> PrestaShop
